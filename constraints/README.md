# PSVS Constraints

This folder describes the main constraints of the Polar Vault Seed Storage System.

## 1. Depositor Registration
Only registered depositors may deposit seed boxes.

## 2. Box Ownership
Every box belongs to exactly one depositor.

## 3. Chamber Capacity
Chambers 1, 2, and 3 can hold at most 50,000 boxes each.
Chamber 4 can hold at most 20,000 boxes.

## 4. Temperature
Chamber temperature must not rise above -18°C, except for the maintenance rule that permits warming to 0°C.

## 5. Annual Withdrawal Quota
A depositor may withdraw at most 20% of its boxes of a species per year.

## 6. Redeposit Rule
A withdrawn box may be redeposited only after re-inspection.

## 7. Emergency Release
The vault authority may release any box to a requesting party during an emergency.

Note: The emergency, temperature, and quota rules require further clarification and verification.
