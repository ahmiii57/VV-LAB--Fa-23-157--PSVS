# PSVS Defect Analysis Report

## 1. Introduction
This report presents the requirement defect analysis of the Polar Vault Seed Storage System (PSVS).

## 2. Objective
The objective is to identify unclear, incomplete, conflicting, and difficult-to-test requirements.

## 3. Analysis Method
The requirements S1-S12 were reviewed using the following defect categories:

- Ambiguity
- Vagueness
- Incompleteness
- Contradiction
- Non-verifiability
- Redundancy
- Infeasibility
- Untraceability

## 4. Identified Defects
A total of 17 candidate defects have been recorded in the defect log.

Three cross-requirement issues were also considered:
- S4 and S5: Ownership and emergency release.
- S7 and S8: Temperature and maintenance.
- S2 and S3: General and specific chamber capacity.

## 5. Recommendations
- Make requirements clear and specific.
- Define measurable limits.
- Clarify emergency exceptions.
- Define maintenance temperature rules.
- Maintain traceability between requirements and defects.

## 6. Conclusion
The analysis helps improve the clarity, consistency, and testability of PSVS requirements. Further review is needed to validate the identified defects.
