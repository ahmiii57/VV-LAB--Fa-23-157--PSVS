# PSVS Defect Taxonomy

## 1. Ambiguity
Definition: A requirement has more than one possible meaning.
Example: S5 does not clearly define an emergency.

## 2. Vagueness
Definition: A requirement uses unclear or non-measurable words.
Example: S12 says "good audit trail".

## 3. Incompleteness
Definition: Important information is missing.
Example: S9 does not define low viability.

## 4. Contradiction
Definition: Two requirements appear to conflict.
Example: S4 versus S5 regarding withdrawal permission.

## 5. Non-verifiability
Definition: A requirement cannot be objectively tested as written.
Example: S6 says "safe temperature" without an exact limit.

## 6. Redundancy
Definition: Two requirements unnecessarily repeat the same information.
Example: S6 and S7 both concern temperature safety; verify whether any information is actually duplicated.

## 7. Infeasibility
Definition: A requirement cannot practically be achieved under the stated constraints.
Example: S7 and S8 need clarification about temperature during maintenance; this alone does not prove infeasibility.

## 8. Untraceability
Definition: A requirement has no recorded source or link to its origin.
Example: The provided requirements do not specify a source reference for each requirement.
